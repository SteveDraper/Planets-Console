Territory movie colors

Does add-order color assignment stay readable when owners are added and removed, and stay stable when only the frame changes?

The first owner you check in a selection gets the first color. Each new owner gets the next color. Owners already checked keep theirs. Unchecking hides their planets, but they keep that color until you start over, and nobody else receives it. Checking them again brings the same color back. Players and league teams each have their own sequence. Changing the turn, switching By team, or switching Owned planets only does not change colors.

Fills are the full baked color, with no outline, and the planet dots are painted on top. That is the recorded drawing rule. The paint buttons can still show the earlier stacking, fills at 35% with the dots underneath, which washed the colors out. Flipping them does not change who has which color.

What the movie is doing now

Judge the fills, not the names. Names are only here so you can tell who is beside whom. The movie itself does not draw names.
